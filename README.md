# Distributed Training Course

A hands-on course teaching distributed training concepts and JAX distributed computing.

## Chapters

1. Why Distributed Training
2. Data Parallelism
3. Model Parallelism
4. Hybrid Parallelism & Communication Primitives
5. Mixed Precision & Fault Tolerance
6. JAX Fundamentals for Distributed Training
7. JAX Sharding & Device Meshes
8. SPMD Programming in JAX
9. Distributed Training with Flax & Optax
10. Multi-host JAX Training in Practice

## Setup



Each chapter is a self-contained Jupyter notebook runnable on Google Colab.
